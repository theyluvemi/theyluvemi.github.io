There are 3 different lines of defence:
Phyiscal barriers such as your skin and mucus.
your skin and mucus act as the very first barrier between pathogens and your cells by blocking and catching pathogens before they can do any harm
Also part of the first line of defence, the non-specific immune response.
the non-specific immune response is called that because it reacts the same way to every pathogen it encounters.
this reaction can include:
  • a fever
  • inflammtion
  • phagocytosis
the non-specific immune response can defeat most infections and this is important because
The specific adaptive immune response.
the final line of defence that takes a hot minute to activate [96 hours] that adapts to specific pathogens and stores memories of the pathogen to easily fight it off again if it returns.
